Frequently asked questions

The script scrolls past me while I am still talking.

Turn on Voice Tracking on the main screen. The script then follows your speaking pace and waits when you pause. You can also adjust the speed with the − and + buttons next to the speed indicator while you read.

Voice tracking does not start in my language.

Some languages have no on-device speech recognition model. For privacy, the app keeps recognition on-device by default, so the feature stays off in those languages. You can allow cloud-based recognition in Settings → Camera; your audio is then processed by Apple's speech service.

Where do my recordings go?

To your Photos library. After you stop recording, a review screen lets you trim the start and the end before saving.

The background replacement looks rough around my hair or shoulders.

Person separation runs on your device and its accuracy depends on lighting. Even, frontal light and clear separation between you and the wall behind you give the best result. Blur is more forgiving than scene backgrounds. You can also switch to Balanced quality in Settings → Camera for cleaner edges at some cost in frame rate.

How do I use another device as a remote?

On the device running the teleprompter choose Wait as Prompter; it shows a device name and a six-digit code. On the second device choose Search as Remote, pick that name and enter the code. Both devices must be on the same local network.

How do I change the language?

Settings → Language.

Privacy

TeleprompterPro collects no data. There is no account, no ads and no analytics, and all image processing runs on your device. The only time anything leaves your device is when you deliberately start a live broadcast to a streaming server you entered yourself. Read the full privacy policy.
