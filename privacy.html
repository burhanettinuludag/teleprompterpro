TeleprompterPro — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

TeleprompterPro is a teleprompter and video recording app for people who speak to a camera. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

Summary: TeleprompterPro collects no data from you. There is no account, no advertising and no analytics. Your videos, your scripts and all image processing stay on your device. The app does not connect to any server operated by us — we do not run one. The only time anything leaves your device is when you deliberately start a live broadcast to a server you yourself entered.

Data we collect

None. We do not collect, store, sell or share any personal data. The app contains no analytics, tracking, advertising or crash-reporting tools. The only third-party code in the app is an open-source streaming library (HaishinKit) used for the optional live broadcast feature described below; it sends nothing anywhere unless you start a broadcast yourself. Its licence is listed inside the app.

Data that stays on your device

Camera and microphone

Camera frames are used to show the teleprompter preview and to record video. All image processing — eye-contact correction, skin smoothing, background replacement and every other filter — runs on your device's own processor. Video frames never leave your device, except while you are deliberately running a live broadcast (see below).

Your recordings

Video is recorded only when you ask for it and is saved to your own Photos library (or to your Movies folder on macOS). We have no access to your recordings and they are never uploaded anywhere.

Your scripts

Scripts you write or save are stored only on your device.

Background images you choose

If you choose one of your own photos as a virtual background, a copy is stored inside the app's own folder on your device. It never leaves the device, and it is deleted when you choose a different image.

Speech recognition

Speech recognition is used to advance the script at your speaking pace. By default this runs on your device only, and your audio never leaves it.

Some languages have no on-device recognition model. In that case the feature simply stays off. You may turn on Allow Cloud-Based Recognition in Settings; only then is audio sent to Apple's speech recognition service for processing, and the app tells you on screen while this is active. That processing is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy. The audio never reaches us.

Live broadcasting (optional)

The app can stream your camera and microphone live to a streaming server — for example the RTMPS address and stream key your own streaming provider gives you. This feature is off until you open the broadcast screen, type in a server address and stream key, and tap the broadcast button.

While a broadcast is running, your video and audio are sent directly from your device to the server you entered. We do not operate that server, we are not a party to the connection, and nothing is routed through us. What happens to your stream once it reaches that server is governed by the privacy policy of whoever runs it, not by this one. The teleprompter text and the on-screen controls are not part of the stream.

The server address and stream key you type are held in memory for that session only. They are not saved to disk, not synced, and not sent anywhere except to the server you entered in order to connect to it. Stream keys are never written into error messages or logs.

Remote control and local network

A second device can be used as a remote control. This works peer-to-peer on your local network only: it uses no internet connection and passes through no server. The two devices pair with a six-digit code, each side proves to the other that it knows that code, and the connection is encrypted. The only information transmitted is play/pause, restart and scroll speed. Your scripts and your video are never transmitted. The name advertised on the network is a random per-session name, not your device's real name.

Permissions the app asks for

  • Camera — teleprompter preview and video recording.
  • Microphone — audio for your recordings, and speech-paced scrolling.
  • Speech recognition — advancing the script as you speak.
  • Photos (add only) — saving your recording to your library. Permission to read your existing photos is never requested.
  • Local network — connecting a nearby device as a remote control.
  • Internet access — used only while you are running a live broadcast to a server you entered. The app makes no other network connections.

You can withdraw any of these permissions at any time in your device settings. Declining a permission disables only the related feature; the rest of the app keeps working.

Children

Because the app collects no data from anyone, it collects no data from children.
